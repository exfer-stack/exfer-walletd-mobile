## What's new

- Meet your in-wallet AI assistant. Open the new Agent tab and just say what you want — "what's my balance?", "send 5 EXFER to …", "swap 0.001 BNB to EXFER", or "mine some EXFER". It does the legwork for you and always shows a plain confirmation you approve before any money moves; decline and nothing is sent.
- Faster, more reliable cross-chain swaps. The new flow locks the pool's side first, so you lock once and can walk away — the swap finishes on its own, and a BNB→EXFER buy now settles in a few minutes instead of much longer. Every step is shown honestly, and if you close the app mid-swap it picks back up and recovers safely when you return.
