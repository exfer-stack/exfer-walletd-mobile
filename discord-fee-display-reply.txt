the real fee is small: a 0.3% liquidity fee plus a fixed 0.0002 BNB network fee (about $0.12). everything above that was price impact, which isn't paid to anyone — it's just the slippage from a trade being large relative to the pool.

we'll fix the display so "fees" shows only the real fee, with price impact on its own separate line instead of mixed in. an update will go out shortly and it'll read correctly.
